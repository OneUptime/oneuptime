extraEnv is appended last, so these still win the REDIS_* slots -- but the app

to the `externalValkey:` block, which is the supported way to point at a cache
this chart does not run. The same applies to any per-service `<service>.extraEnv`
and to a docker-compose.override.yml that sets REDIS_* by hand.
